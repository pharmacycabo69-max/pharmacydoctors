Good to know

Rapid Hydration, answered

What guests usually ask before booking their drip.

How fast will I feel the difference?
Fluids go straight into your bloodstream, so relief does not wait on digestion. Most guests start feeling better before the bag is finished, with the full effect settling in over the following hours.
How long does the session take?
Plan for 45 to 60 minutes from setup to finish. Our nurse typically arrives within about 45 minutes anywhere in Cabo San Lucas or San José del Cabo, and stays with you for the entire drip.
Where can you set up the IV?
Almost anywhere you are comfortable: hotel and resort suites, private villas, yachts at the marina and vacation rentals across Cabo San Lucas, the Tourist Corridor and San José del Cabo. Prefer to come to us? Walk into our downtown clinic.
Who administers the drip?
A COFEPRIS-licensed nurse checks your vitals, reviews a short intake and administers the IV with sealed, single-use supplies. A physician reviews every case and remains on standby throughout your visit.
Can I book it for pregnancy nausea?
Gentle rehydration is one of our most requested visits during pregnancy. The included physician consultation confirms it is appropriate for you first, and we always recommend keeping your own doctor in the loop.
How do I pay — and does insurance help?
We accept USD, MXN and major cards, with clear pricing from $129 USD. You receive an itemized invoice you can submit to your travel insurance for possible reimbursement.
