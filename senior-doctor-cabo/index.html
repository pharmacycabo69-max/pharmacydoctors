About Our Geriatric Doctor Service

Our English-speaking geriatric doctor provides compassionate, personalized care for seniors in Cabo San Lucas and San José del Cabo. We understand the challenges older adults may face when traveling or living abroad, which is why we deliver high-quality, private healthcare directly to your home, villa, condo, or resort.

Each visit focuses on safety, comfort, and clear communication with both the patient and their family. Our assessment includes medication review, chronic disease management, mobility evaluation, cognitive screening, and preventive care—following the same standards used in the United States and Canada.

How Our Geriatric Home Visit Works

Our process is simple, safe, and designed to give families peace of mind. Here’s how our geriatric home visit service works:

  1. Request a Visit – Contact us and provide basic details about the patient’s condition, location, and preferred time.

  2. Doctor is Assigned – We send an English-speaking geriatric specialist to your home, villa, condo, or resort.

  3. Comprehensive Evaluation – The doctor performs a full medical assessment, reviews medications, checks vitals, and evaluates mobility, memory, and overall health.

  4. Care Plan & Follow-Up – You receive a clear care plan, medication order management, and ongoing communication. We also coordinate updates with family members in the U.S. or Canada when needed.
