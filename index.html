Safety first

Careful medicine, not a menu

Every visit starts with a professional evaluation. That is what separates private medical care from a delivery service.

Chest pain, trouble breathing, fainting, confusion or a severe injury: call 911 first.

  • Health questions first. A licensed nurse asks about your health, medicines and allergies; a doctor reviews when needed.
  • Sterile, single-use supplies opened in front of you.
  • We may say no. If an IV or visit is not right for you, we recommend another option.
  • Pregnancy, heart or kidney disease, blood thinners: tell us before booking.

FAQ

IV therapy in Cabo: common questions

Do you have group discounts?

Yes. Groups of 6 or more people get IV drips for $119 USD per person, tax included, at the same villa, hotel or yacht — popular for bachelorette parties, weddings and villa groups. You can book from the US before your trip. See group IV →

How fast can a nurse get to me in Cabo?

We confirm the arrival time when you book. Send your location on WhatsApp and we will confirm the exact time before the nurse heads out.

How much does IV therapy cost in Cabo San Lucas?

Our IV drips cost $159 USD, tax included, delivered to your villa, hotel, Airbnb or yacht, or $129 USD, tax included, at our downtown clinic in Cabo San Lucas. Cards are accepted.

Who gives the IV? Is it safe?

A licensed nurse from our team gives every IV, using sealed medical-grade ingredients and a sterile, single-use set. If an IV is not right for you, we will tell you and can arrange a doctor visit instead.

Can I get an IV at my hotel, villa or yacht?

Yes. We come to hotel rooms, private villas, Airbnbs and yachts at the Marina across Los Cabos, including Pedregal, Palmilla, the Tourist Corridor and San José del Cabo.

Do you also send doctors and deliver medications?

Yes. An English-speaking doctor can visit you 24/7 ($189 USD per visit, or $99 USD at our downtown clinic), and we deliver medications and pharmacy essentials to your door. One WhatsApp message covers all three.

Can a group book IVs together?

Yes. Group sessions at villas and rentals are common. Tell us how many people and which drips, and we will send the right number of nurses.

Do you speak English?

Yes. Our team speaks English and Spanish, on WhatsApp, on the phone and in person.

How do I pay?

Prices are in USD and cards are accepted. Your nurse confirms the total before starting.

Feeling rough? We can be there today.

Tell us where you are and what you need. A licensed nurse can come to your door — we confirm the arrival time when you book.
