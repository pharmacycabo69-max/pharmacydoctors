Frequently Asked

Questions visitors ask before booking

How is Myers different from Rapid Hydration?
Hydration is fast (45 min) and focused on fluids + electrolytes. Myers is a 60-min full wellness boost with B-complex, B12, magnesium, and Vitamin C for energy, immunity, and recovery.
How often can I get a Myers IV?
Most guests do once every 2-4 weeks for ongoing support. Some get it weekly during recovery periods, heavy travel, or peak training blocks.
Where do you deliver in Los Cabos?
Cabo San Lucas, San José del Cabo, Pedregal, Palmilla, Punta Ballena, plus hotels, villas, and yachts marina-side. ETA is typically 30-45 minutes.
Do you accept USD?
Yes. USD pricing is shown on this page. We also accept credit cards and Mexican pesos at the clinic.
Is it safe? What’s your credentialing?
COFEPRIS-licensed nurses, sealed medical-grade ingredients sourced from accredited Mexican distributors, and a Mexican-licensed physician oversees the protocol.
Can I add Glutathione or NAD+?
Yes. Add-ons available at booking. Glutathione push +$45 USD. NAD+ push +$95 USD. Confirm with the RN at intake.
