Medical Concierge on the Tourist Corridor, Los Cabos

The Tourist Corridor — the twenty miles between Cabo San Lucas and San José del Cabo — is where most of the luxury inventory in Los Cabos actually sits: Esperanza, Las Ventanas, Montage, Chileno Bay, Cape Thompson, Waldorf Astoria Pedregal at the western end, Hilton, Westin, Marquis, Marbella, JW, Le Blanc, and a long list of private estates between them. Driving down to a downtown clinic for a single appointment is a non-starter. Our concierge is built to come to your resort.

COFEPRIS-licensed · #05-022-BEnglish-speaking MDs & RNs24/7 concierge dispatchPrivate · discreet · no deposit

Why corridor guests skip the clinic and use a concierge

The corridor was designed for resort stays, not for getting in and out of town. A consultation at a downtown clinic is at least an hour each way from a property like Esperanza or Las Ventanas — and that is before you sit in a waiting room. Our concierge takes that round trip off your day. The physician arrives at your resort or villa, completes the visit in your suite, issues any medication order on the spot, and coordinates pharmacy delivery to the front desk before leaving.

For IV therapy, in-room delivery is the standard, not the exception. A registered nurse brings the kit, runs the drip on your balcony or in bed, and is gone in about an hour. Pharmacy orders ride along the same dispatch — a single window covers consultation, medication order, and medication.

Pharmacy & Doctors operates under COFEPRIS pharmacy authorization #05-022-B and is reachable 24/7 across both ends of the corridor.

Concierge services delivered along the corridor

Same menu across every resort. The team adapts to the property — front-desk check-in at Chileno Bay, butler routing at Las Ventanas, casita meet-up at Esperanza, gate clearance at Montage, lobby concierge at the Hilton or Westin.

Private House-Call Doctor

Mexican-licensed, English-speaking MDs for in-suite evaluation, urgent care, medication orders, sutures, and second opinions at any corridor resort or villa.

Mobile IV Therapy

Bespoke hydration, hangover, NAD, immunity, vitamin, and athletic recovery drips administered by a registered nurse in your suite, casita, or pool cabana.

Pharmacy Delivery

Medication order and OTC medication couriered to the front desk, your villa, or directly to the room — same-day, often within the hour, across every corridor resort.

In-Suite Nurse Care

Wound care, IV medications, post-op monitoring, and bedside nursing for one visit or a full multi-day stay across a wedding week, medical-tourism recovery, or family trip.

How a corridor concierge visit is dispatched

The corridor is long, but the request flow is the same wherever you are staying.

  1. Reach the concierge. WhatsApp, call, or fill out the request form. Give us your resort, suite or villa number, what is happening, and a time window.
  2. We confirm and route. A coordinator confirms timing, dispatches the doctor, nurse, or IV team from the closest base, and texts you the name and arrival time. Pharmacy orders move through our COFEPRIS-licensed pharmacy in parallel.
  3. Care arrives at your resort. The doctor or nurse checks in at the front desk, with your butler, or directly at your villa gate, and cares for you inside your suite. Medication orders are issued in person and delivery is coordinated before they leave.

Service area: every resort, every kilometer of the corridor

We routinely dispatch to: Waldorf Astoria Pedregal, Esperanza, an Auberge Resort; Las Ventanas al Paraíso, a Rosewood Resort; Montage Los Cabos; Chileno Bay Resort; Cape Thompson Hotel; Nobu Hotel Los Cabos; Hard Rock Hotel; Le Blanc Spa Resort; Grand Velas; Garza Blanca; JW Marriott Los Cabos; Hilton Los Cabos; Westin Los Cabos; Marquis Los Cabos; Marbella Suites en la Playa; Viceroy Los Cabos; Krystal Grand; Dreams Los Cabos; Secrets Puerto Los Cabos; Hacienda Encantada; Hyatt Ziva Los Cabos; Riu Palace Baja California; and the residential estates of Cabo del Sol, Querencia, and Palmilla in between.

If your property is along the Transpeninsular, we cover it. Send the resort name on WhatsApp and we will confirm dispatch within minutes.

COFEPRIS-licensed care, English-speaking team

Every physician dispatched along the corridor is Mexican-licensed and fluent in English. Every registered nurse is board-certified. Medication orders are written only after the clinical consultation, only by a licensed Mexican MD, and never for restricted medications on request.

Our pharmacy operates under COFEPRIS #05-022-B (license printed on our home page). We issue itemized English-language invoices accepted by international travel-medical insurers for reimbursement. Payment is at the time of service by card or transfer.

Questions guests ask

Which corridor resorts do you serve?
All of them. We routinely dispatch to Esperanza, Las Ventanas, Montage, Chileno Bay, Cape Thompson, Nobu, Waldorf Astoria, Hilton, Westin, Marquis, JW Marriott, Grand Velas, Le Blanc, Hard Rock, Garza Blanca, Marbella, Viceroy, Krystal Grand, Dreams, Secrets, Hacienda Encantada, and Hyatt Ziva — plus the private estates between them.
How quickly can a doctor or IV team reach a corridor resort?
Standard dispatch is 30 to 60 minutes depending on which end of the corridor you are on. Urgent symptoms are prioritized; we are available 24/7 including weekends and holidays.
Can the doctor issue a medication order and have it delivered at my resort?
Yes. After the in-suite consultation, the physician issues a Mexican medication order; our COFEPRIS-licensed pharmacy then dispatches the medication to your front desk or room — often before the doctor leaves the property.
Do you coordinate with the resort concierge or butler?
Yes. Many of our corridor visits are routed through the property concierge, butler, or assistant manager. We are accustomed to working through whichever channel you and the property prefer.
Do you issue insurance-friendly invoices?
Yes. We provide itemized English-language invoices accepted by major travel insurers (Allianz, GeoBlue, IMG, Cigna Global). We do not bill insurance directly; payment is at the time of service by card or transfer.
