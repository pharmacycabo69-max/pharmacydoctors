Before you book

Good to know

  • Results vary from person to person and are not guaranteed.
  • Some treatments are not advised during pregnancy or breastfeeding, or with certain medical conditions.
  • Sun, swimming, alcohol and flights right after a treatment can matter — plan your trip with the doctor.
  • We do not publish before-and-after photos without the patient’s written permission.
At your evaluation

What to bring and tell us

  • The treatment you are considering and the dates you are in Cabo.
  • Any medicines, supplements and allergies — and if you are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Past aesthetic treatments, even if they were done elsewhere.
  • Questions: benefits, risks, recovery time and the total price.

FAQ

Aesthetic medicine in Cabo: common questions

How much are lip fillers in Cabo San Lucas?

Lip fillers with hyaluronic acid are $600 USD, always after an in-person evaluation.

How much is Botox in Cabo?

Botox for the upper face (frown lines, forehead and crow’s feet) is $550 USD. Smaller facial areas start at $170 USD.

What does facial rejuvenation include?

Options range from NCTF deep hydration ($300) and polynucleotides ($350) to collagen stimulation with Radiesse ($1,000) or Sculptra ($1,200). The evaluation decides what suits you.

How much is skin brightening in Cabo?

The Cosmelan depigmenting peel for dark spots is $1,500 USD. Strict sun protection afterwards is essential.

Who performs the treatments?
