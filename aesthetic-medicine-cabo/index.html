How it works

From first message to aftercare

  1. Message usTell us the treatment you are interested in and the dates you are in Cabo.
  2. EvaluationDra. Rosibel reviews your goals and health, explains benefits, risks, recovery and the total price.
  3. TreatmentOnly if it is right for you, at our downtown clinic on Francisco I. Madero.
  4. AftercareWritten instructions and a direct line to the team for your questions.

Results vary from person to person and are not guaranteed. Some treatments are not advised during pregnancy or breastfeeding, with certain medical conditions, or right before intense sun, swimming or a flight — plan your trip with the doctor.

FAQ

Aesthetic medicine in Cabo: common questions

How much are lip fillers in Cabo San Lucas?

Lip augmentation and contouring with hyaluronic acid is $600 USD. A 3-syringe package to combine areas is $1,600 USD. Treatment is always after an evaluation.

How much is Botox in Cabo?

Facial botulinum toxin for the upper face (frown lines, forehead and crow’s feet) is $550 USD. Smaller areas start at $170 USD.

Who performs the treatments?

Our aesthetic medicine division is led by Dra. Rosibel Núñez Rojo, Médico Cirujano (UAG), Cédula Profesional 7408277.

Can I get treated while on vacation?

Often yes, but timing matters. The doctor will tell you whether sun, swimming, alcohol or a flight soon after the treatment is a problem.

Is there an evaluation fee?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp and we confirm the evaluation fee and an appointment time.
